How We Get to Our Pricing

Our pricing is reflected by our lean, user-centric approach to product innovation and software development. This, combined with top industry talent, means our small focused teams deliver projects on time and on budget.

Our Seed Program accelerates delivery by using a modular foundation. With core modules ready, we focus on your unique features, integrations and UI — reducing time to MVP while keeping quality high.

We work in short iterations, clear milestones and transparent communication. You get visibility on scope, progress and risk, while we protect delivery through strong engineering standards.

Teams are sized to match your project. Typically: Product + Design + Engineering. We scale up or down as needed, keeping momentum without wasting budget.

Seed Project Explained

Our Seed program is a purpose-agnostic app with a highly modular microservices architecture composed by a group of components that, when combined, can be the foundation of a project. With the seed in place, the project starts with ~50% of the code already done, while we can focus on developing the CORE: custom functionalities and interfaces.

Seed Modules

Most projects have a common ground — functionalities/modules that are always necessary: email services, signup with email/social networks, password recovery, email validation flows, etc. That is exactly what the seed covers and is reflected in our pricing.
